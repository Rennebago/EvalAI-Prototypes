## **Overview of the wholesale compliant process**

### Level 1: Regulations Reviews Oct 15, 2026

**SME:** Ryanne Romo

* How do complaints live in the wholesale process?  
* Levels/Tiers:  
  * 1 \- regulatory, such as fdac, department of state (primary levels she handles) \- 5 in Oct., 13-14/mo, unsure which were wholesale specific  
  * 2 \- directed CEO, CFOs at brad s, sean fox, etc (not often)  
  * 3 \- business level, bbb  
  * 4 \- customer service   
* Handles (manually) regulatory complaints (level 1), FDR, all entities (wholesale is FDR specific)  
  * Review calls what was said, or promised  
  * Sometimes texts are involved  
  * Level 1 complaints can come in via mail or portal, some are retail  
    * Month of Oct. we had 5 wholesale level 1 (regulatory)  
* Make notes in Word and send to WS to address with the partner  
  * Expectation on partner (not sure, doesn’t see that side)  
  * Send floor notifications, reminders, notifications, if bad enough; termination  
  * Should see more response on corrective actions, as of Oct. 1  
* Review which partner has enrolled a customer, finds dc on the file and requests calls  
* Legal through “high Q” \- sometimes listen to a wave of mp3 file  
  * Renewed with them for another year  
  * Handles documentation and review  
* Want to review the transcripts/listen to the calls  
* Obtaining complaints: regulatory agency sends to her  
  * Pulls everything and gets review together  
* **Examples:**  
  * Credit impact  
  * FDR program is more income-based loan, mention of ‘loan’  
  * Verbiage telling clients to rack up their cards, or charge accounts, or ‘maximize the profit’ language  
  * Litigation language: ‘You won't be sued’ or “this company will go to court for you” language \- we don’t do this  
* She would be more interested in just what she needs to review  
  * Compliance may be interested in the whole view  
* Resolutions:  
  * Not agreeable, then respond to complain  
  * Monetary \- help prevent further escalation  
* Usually closed on agencies end when a response is received

### Customer Service Oct 15, 2026

**SME:** Christy Hallett

* Work complaints from Salesforce and Genesys  
  * Sometimes use CFT website \- generate ledgers to send to clients  
* Her team handles level 2 \- hardly get level 2  
  * One person handles level 1  
  * They don’t have a level 4  
  * Escalated level 3  
* Pull calls from genesys  
  * Some agents have access (team changes) some don’t  
  * Audio and read through transcripts \- esp read when they have long 2 hour calls  
    * In this case, they use Gemini to summarize and ask specific questions \- where in the call were fees discussed? Was xyz said? \- then the team goes to that section and listen  
* **Wholesale** gets tricky \- need to reach out to WS mgmt, WS reaches out to partners, then Christy’s team waits for response (Most common complaints come from Symple, Penni, RescueOne)  
  * Can take up to a week to a month+  
  * Working to change this process, looped in with compliance  
  * They don’t get the whole call: When WS gets call from partner, WS then listens to the calls and they provide a summary to customer service teams  
    * Sometimes has to go back to WS to request more info  
  * If we have to do a big refund, it’s recouped from the partner  
  * *Her team really wants the ability to review the calls themselves \- they have confidence in the WS team but sometimes things get missed and makes it difficult to resolve with the client*  
  * Issue with not receiving the actual call \- makes it hard to actually confirm the situation, since they don’t have access to the actual recording or transcript  
* Client calls and if agent isn’t able to resolve, call is transferred to coach line  
* Uses salesforce to sort through complaints \- Client Services Consumer  
  * Mostly use SF \- only time we look at SUIP, sometimes get clients who are not fully enrolled  
    * Were they in the process of enrollment  
    * Typically complaints against the DC  
* Supervisor coach complaint (level 3\)  
  * Fill out complaint details, source, reason, and details  
  * This helps them track trends over time  
  * Saves to client profile  
    * Holds any previous complaints

* If client demands refund, threaten to go to BBB \- Christy’s team takes over, looks at what happened, listen to calls, dig into accounts, then begin outbound attempts to contact the client  
  * 3 attempts \- if no answer or follow up, close the complaint (unfortunate, we want to help resolve)  
* Sometimes they’re mad since they take out an AAL loan and feel like FDR passed them off  
* Follow up FLA (SLA?) \- if a complaint is open on monday with escalations, gets reassigned on tuesday. They have 2 days to reach out to the client upon receipt.
